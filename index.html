Animal husbandry, done with care

Gazellix LLC is an animal husbandry company based in Sheridan, Wyoming. We work with producers to raise animals responsibly, keep herds productive and make daily farm decisions easier.

Every farm is different. We start with your land, your animals and your goals, then build a plan that fits.

  • Animal welfare comes first
  • Practical advice you can use on the farm
  • Plans built around your operation
  • Clear, honest communication

What we do

From the first breeding decision to the daily feed ration, we support each stage of raising livestock.

Livestock breeding

Breeding programs that improve herd quality, fertility and long-term performance.

Feed and nutrition

Rations matched to each animal’s age, purpose and season, so feed goes further.

Herd health

Prevention routines, monitoring and record keeping that catch problems early.

Pasture management

Grazing plans that protect your land and keep forage growing season after season.

Housing and handling

Low-stress handling and housing advice that keeps animals calm and workers safe.

Farm consulting

Hands-on guidance for new and established producers who want to grow with confidence.

How we work with you

A simple process, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Listen

    We learn about your animals, land and goals.

  2. Assess

    We review herd health, feed and pasture conditions.

  3. Plan

    You get a clear plan with priorities and timelines.

  4. Support

    We follow up and adjust as your farm changes.

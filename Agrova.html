Privacy Policy

App: Agrova (Android)  ·  Developer: Gazellix  ·  Effective date: November 4, 2026

In short: Agrova works fully offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It requests no device permissions, contains no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Agrova mobile application (“Agrova”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. Agrova is a livestock record-keeping tool published by Gazellix. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Agrova has no servers, no user accounts and no connection to the internet, so we never receive the data you enter into the app.

The records you create (for example animal tags, species, breeds, birth dates, health records, weigh-ins, breeding records, feed calculations and your app settings) are saved only in the app’s private storage on your own device.

3. Permissions

Agrova requests no Android permissions. In particular, it does not access:

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone or camera
  • Your photos, files or other storage
  • The internet or network state
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID

4. Internet use and third-party services

The app does not connect to the internet. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-media or any other third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not sell, rent, trade or share data with anyone, because we have no access to it.

5. Data stored on your device

Your data is stored locally and is accessible only to the app on your device, under the protection of the Android operating system. You are responsible for the security of your device (for example by using a screen lock).

6. Device backup

Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in automatic backups to your own Google account or device-transfer tools. Those backups are created and controlled by the operating system and by you. Gazellix does not have access to them. You can manage or disable backups in your device settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase all animal, health, weight and breeding records at any time from Settings → Data → Erase all data, delete individual animals inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Agrova. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted or if you lose your device.

8. Children’s privacy

Agrova is a farm-management tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data about you, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, export or delete. All of your information is under your control on your device. If you have any privacy question or request, please contact us using the details below.

10. Veterinary and planning disclaimer

Feed amounts, due dates and growth figures shown in Agrova are general estimates intended to help you organize your records. They are not veterinary, nutritional or professional advice. Please consult a qualified veterinarian or animal nutritionist for decisions about animal health and feeding.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app adds new features. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated before that change is released.

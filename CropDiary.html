1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the CropDiary mobile application (“CropDiary”, “the app”) published by Gazellix (“we”, “us”, “our”). CropDiary is an offline farm and field journal that helps you organise fields, crop cycles, tasks, activity records, harvests, expenses and inventory.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app, and the app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. Specifically, CropDiary:

  • does not require an account, registration or login;
  • does not connect to the internet or make network requests;
  • does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or notifications;
  • does not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the information that you choose to enter. This information is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Farm recordsField names and codes, area, soil and irrigation type, location descriptions you type, notes.
Crop recordsCrop cycles, varieties, dates, growth stages, expected and actual yields.
Daily recordsActivities, irrigation, rainfall, fertilizer and chemical applications, inspections, tasks.
Financial recordsHarvest quantities, selling prices, buyer names (optional), expenses, inventory and stock history.
PreferencesYour display name, accent color, units, currency and date format.

Location descriptions are free text that you type yourself; the app never reads your device location. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information you do not want stored on your device.

4. Permissions

CropDiary does not request any Android permissions. It does not ask for access to storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, notifications or the internet.

5. Third parties and SDKs

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no data is shared with third parties. We do not sell personal information.

6. Device backups

Depending on your device and settings, the Android operating system may include app data in your device backup (for example, a backup to your Google account). Such backups are managed by your device and its provider under their own terms and privacy policies; we do not receive or have access to them. You can control backups in your device’s system settings.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can edit or delete any individual record inside the app.
  • You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Delete all data.
  • You can also clear the app’s storage in your Android system settings, or uninstall the app, which removes the data stored by the app on your device.

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot recover it for you if you delete it or lose your device.

8. Children’s privacy

CropDiary is a general-purpose farming tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Because the app does not transmit data, nothing is sent over a network. Keeping your device secure (screen lock, system updates) helps protect the information stored on it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. Since we do not collect or hold your personal data and do not sell or share it, there is no data for us to provide or delete — you remain in full control of everything stored in the app on your device. If you have a question about your rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Important notice about estimates

CropDiary is a record-keeping tool. Crop progress is a calendar-based estimate and is not a measurement of biological growth. Financial figures, including estimated profit, are calculated only from the records you enter. The app does not measure soil moisture and does not recommend irrigation, fertilizers, pesticides or doses. Always follow product labels, local regulations and the advice of a qualified professional.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app changes. The updated version will be posted at the same address with a new “Last updated” date. If we ever add features that handle personal data, we will update this policy before releasing them and, where required, ask for your consent.
